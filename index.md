## About Me

I’m a **Master of Nursing (Nurse Practitioner — Family/All Ages)** student at the **University of Alberta** (GPA 4.0/4.0) and an **Assistant Head Nurse** in Family & Urgent Care at the East Edmonton Health Centre. 

With **12,500+ clinical hours** spanning urban trauma centres, urgent care, and rural Northern Alberta emergency departments, my work sits at the intersection of **frontline clinical excellence, health technology, and nursing education**. Whether I’m teaching ultrasound-guided IV access, investigating the ethics of AI scribes in emergency medicine, or exploring existentialist frameworks for nurse burnout, I’m driven by one question: *how do we build healthcare systems where both patients and clinicians can thrive?*
